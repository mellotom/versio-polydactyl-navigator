# versio-polydactyl-navigator

travelers guide for fine tuning your hyperdrive: a one-page reference for **polydactyl versio**, the noise engineering three-band dynamics firmware for the versio platform. every knob by its number on the numbered panel, the crossovers, room, the gate and the leds drawn out, patches and specs.

source: the noise engineering polydactyl versio manual.

part of the [versio starsystem](https://mellotom.github.io/versio-navigator/) in the [travelers guide welcome center](https://mellotom.github.io/travelers-guide/).
